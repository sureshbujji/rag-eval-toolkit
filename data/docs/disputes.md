# Disputes

A dispute (chargeback) is opened when a customer contests a charge with their bank. You are notified by email and a `dispute.created` webhook.

You have 21 days to submit evidence: receipts, shipping tracking, and customer communication. The dispute amount plus a $15 fee is held from your balance while the dispute is open.

Win rates improve with complete evidence bundles. If you accept a dispute, the funds are returned to the customer and the case closes as `lost`. Prevent disputes with clear billing descriptors and prompt support responses.
